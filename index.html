AI Home Hub Backup is a personal desktop utility being configured to store locally encrypted backup files in its owner's Google Drive.

What the app does

The utility uses Restic to encrypt selected files locally and rclone to transfer encrypted repository objects to Google Drive. Its purpose is backup and recovery. This information page does not upload files or connect to Google accounts.

Google access

The app requests access only to Drive files it creates or that its owner explicitly makes available to it, using Google's drive.file permission. Account authorization happens through Google's consent screen on the owner's computer.

This is an owner-operated personal tool with no public registration or download. Read the privacy notice for its intended data handling and how access can be revoked.
